Rangeway and Maggie's ReFuel are pairing fast charging with Maggie's food and retail, starting at a Beverly Hills landmark.

**BEVERLY HILLS, Calif., [Release Date]** — Rangeway and Maggie's ReFuel are partnering to bring ChargeVia by Rangeway fast charging to Maggie's locations, beginning with a planned project at the corner of Santa Monica Boulevard and N Crescent Drive in Beverly Hills.

Maggie's ReFuel was founded by Alex Canter, its CEO and a fourth-generation member of the family behind Los Angeles' Canter's Deli. At the Beverly Hills location, Maggie's is reimagining the neighborhood gas station beneath its sweeping mid-century canopy, with a food program featuring lattes, matcha and soft-serve, and shelves stocked with local, regional and national brands. Charging is planned as one more reason for EV drivers to stop in.

“Charging should fit into the places people already want to stop,” said Zak Winnick, founder and CEO of Rangeway. “What Alex and the team are creating at Maggie's is special. They're taking a location with a lot of character and giving people a new reason to spend time there. We're excited to work with them to make EV charging part of that experience.”

Pull in behind Maggie's, plug in and make yourself at home. Grab a latte, matcha or a bite to eat and enjoy Maggie's planned outdoor patio while you charge. Prefer to stay in the car? Charger screens will feature a QR code connecting drivers to Maggie's menu, with ordering for pickup inside or delivery right to the car. The planned charging area will be accessible from the alley connecting Santa Monica Boulevard and Brighton Way.

The partnership is intended to support charging at Maggie's ReFuel locations beyond Beverly Hills, with additional sites to be evaluated together. ChargeVia by Rangeway is Rangeway's fast-charging format for retail, urban and other host locations, designed to connect fast charging with the businesses and amenities already there.

Rangeway and Maggie's will continue working through site design and development and will share additional details, including an anticipated opening date, as plans progress.

### About Rangeway

Rangeway is building a network of EV charging stops designed around what drivers need while they're there. Its formats range from hospitality-driven charging destinations in development to ChargeVia by Rangeway, a fast-charging format for host properties.
