A comprehensive guide to the Rangeway brand identity, visual system, and communication standards.

01

Brand Foundation

The strategic foundation that guides everything we do and how we present ourselves to the world.

Travel farther. Stop better.

Rangeway is a hospitality-first premium EV charging network. Sage gives the identity a grounded setting, and cream keeps it warm and welcoming.

Waystations and Basecamps

Waystations are automated and unstaffed. Basecamps are staffed.

Summit and Lookouts

Use Summit on its own. Guest accommodations are Lookouts.

ChargeVia by Rangeway

ChargeVia by Rangeway is the primary product expression. ChargeVia remains a Rangeway product and host-site format.

Logo System

The integrated R begins the name. The amber dot inside the R is the path mark. Use the supplied wordmark, and do not place the standalone R next to it.

Primary Logo

The primary logo is the integrated wordmark. On sage, the primary brand field, use the cream wordmark with the amber path mark. On cream, the main light canvas, use the charcoal wordmark with the amber path mark.

Clear Space

Leave at least one path-mark diameter of clear space on all sides of the visible logo. Keep the background quiet, and do not place text inside this area.

App Icon

The primary avatar and app icon is the sage-field R. It is the same artwork as the favicon. The standalone R is for small labels, repeated brand details, and other compact avatar uses.

Sage-field R

Minimum Sizes

Working minimums are 140px wide for the wordmark and 24px for the standalone R. Check the artwork at final size. The 16px favicon is the compact exception.

Incorrect Usage

Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, redraw the lettering, recolor the dot independently, or crop the R. Do not place the standalone R next to the wordmark.

Contrast

Use charcoal text on cream or amber, and cream text on sage or charcoal. Avoid amber for small text on cream. Check contrast whenever adding a new combination. Print conversions depend on paper, ink, and the printer profile, so approve a physical proof before production.

05

Voice & Tone

Write directly and warmly. Describe the guest experience in concrete terms.

Brand Voice

The public name is Rangeway. Reserve Rangeway Energy, Inc. for legal or formal contexts. Use lowercase driver's lounge and hotel operators.

Direct

Say what the experience is, in plain language.

Warm

Sound welcoming. Write the way a host would speak to a guest.

Concrete

Describe the guest experience in concrete terms. Skip inflated claims.

Language Guidelines

Use This Language

  • Rangeway
  • driver's lounge
  • hotel operators
  • Waystation, Basecamp, and Summit
  • Lookouts for guest accommodations
  • ChargeVia by Rangeway
  • Pathfinder Rewards, still pre-launch

Avoid This Language

  • Em dashes
  • Inflated claims
  • Generic utility language
  • Rangeway Energy, Inc. outside legal or formal contexts
  • Rangeway Summit
